Common questions

How do I generate study notes?

Open a YouTube lecture and click AI Study Notes in the Chrome toolbar. Choose Fetch YouTube Transcript or paste the transcript manually. Select your notes language and style, then click Generate Notes.

Why can't the extension fetch my transcript?

On the YouTube watch page, look for Show transcript. If it is available, open it and try again, or copy the text into the extension. If the video has no transcript, you can use your own lecture text.

Why didn't my notes generate?

Check your internet connection and make sure the transcript box contains lecture text. If the extension shows a daily limit, try again after the quota resets.

How do timestamps and exports work?

Provide a YouTube link and a transcript with timestamps to jump from a note to the video. Use the Markdown option to copy formatted text. For a PDF, choose Save as PDF in the print dialog.

Why isn't my Pro key working?

Open Manage Key and check for extra spaces. If you see an expiration or device-limit error, use the support channel listed in your purchase instructions. Keep your Pro key private.
