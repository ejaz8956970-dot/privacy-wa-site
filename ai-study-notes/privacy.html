Summary: AI Study Notes only processes the lecture transcript you provide to generate study notes in your chosen language, and tracks basic usage/device limits to keep Free and Pro plans fair. We do not sell your personal data.

📄1. Information We Process

  • Lecture Transcripts & URLs: When you click Generate Notes, the transcript text (up to 60,000 characters) and optional YouTube link are sent to our backend API so AI can structure notes, timestamps, quick revisions, and key terms.
  • Client & Device Identifier: A randomly generated local ID (deviceId) is used to track daily free usage (5 notes/day) and enforce the Pro license limit of up to 2 active devices per Pro key.
  • Pro License Key & Usage Counts: When activating a Pro key, your key and daily usage count are verified against our cloud database so your Pro quota stays accurate across sessions.
  • Local Browser Storage: Preferences such as your selected notes language, style, saved Pro key, and daily count cache are stored locally in your browser via localStorage.

⚙️2. How We Use Information

We use processed data strictly to:

  • Generate study notes, summaries, glossaries, and PDF/Markdown exports in your selected language.
  • Enforce daily generation limits for Free and Pro users and prevent unauthorized Pro key sharing across excessive devices.
  • Maintain reliability, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot service errors.

💳3. Third-Party Services & Payments

  • AI & Hosting Infrastructure: Backend requests are processed through our API server and cloud database infrastructure to generate notes and verify Pro keys.
  • Payments: International ($5/month) and local Pakistan (Rs. 1,000/month) payments are handled via their respective checkout/payment methods. Payment card details are never stored inside the Chrome extension.

🔐4. Data Retention & Security

Lecture transcripts are processed to generate your requested notes rather than published publicly. All communication between the extension and backend API occurs over encrypted HTTPS connections.

✉️5. Your Choices & Contact

You can clear your saved preferences, Pro key, and local usage data anytime by clearing site/extension storage or removing the extension from Chrome.
