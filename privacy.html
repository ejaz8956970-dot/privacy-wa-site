Privacy Policy

Product: Privacy WA — Chat Blur & Quick Reply
Publisher: Tron Business (tronbusiness.shop)
Effective Date: November 2026

1. Local Data Storage

Privacy WA stores all user preferences, blur settings, quick reply templates, and optional chat order tags/notes locally inside your browser profile using chrome.storage.local. We do not operate remote servers to collect or read your WhatsApp messages.

2. PIN Lock & Recovery Code

When you configure a PIN lock, your PIN and recovery code are processed locally in your browser using cryptographic SHA-256 hashing. Plaintext PINs are never transmitted outside your device.

3. Manual JSON Backup Exports

If you choose to export a backup file from the extension popup, the generated JSON file is saved directly to your device and contains your quick replies and chat notes in plain text so you can restore them later. Please store exported backup files securely.

4. Permissions Used

  • storage: Saves your blur preferences, PIN hash, quick replies, and order tags locally.
  • tabs / activeTab: Applies privacy blur controls and quick replies exclusively on web.whatsapp.com.

5. Contact

For privacy questions or support, visit Support & Guide or reach us via tronbusiness.shop.
